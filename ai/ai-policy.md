# Safe AI Use in This Project

We use AI tools to **help us**, not to replace us. The goal is to get support with small tasks while keeping full control over the project.

## What AI Can Do
- Make small code tweaks or improvements.
- Write or update tests.
- Help with styling or formatting.
- Suggest simple fixes or refactors.

## What We Must Do
- **Always review AI changes.** Don’t trust them blindly.
- **Avoid slop.** Every line must be clean, clear, and make sense.
- **Use AI to assist, not to do the full job.**
- **Keep quality first.** If AI adds noise or unclear code, remove or rewrite it.

## How We Stay Safe
- Check everything before committing.
- Log important prompts and AI changes in `ai/prompt-history.md`.
- Make sure we understand every piece of code added or changed.
